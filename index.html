Day-by-day itinerary

Reconfirm exact departure timings, road conditions and stops with each group lead before departure.

DAY 01 · 13 NOV

Group A to Goa

05:00 AM departure from Bangalore. NH48 via Hubballi–Dharwad and the Anmod approach, with fuel, breakfast and lunch breaks. Estimated arrival 4–6 PM, then hotel check-in and dinner.

DAY 02 · 14 NOV

Groups regroup

Group B rides from Bangalore and Group C from Pune. Group A makes the transition ride. Regroup at Regenta Baywatch Resort, South Goa.

DAY 03 · 15 NOV

South Goa beach loop

Suggested schedule: breakfast 8 AM, ride 9 AM, lunch, beach time and sunset dinner. Loop: Colva → Benaulim → Varca → Cavelossim → Mobor → resort.

DAY 04 · 16 NOV

Return journeys

Groups A/B return via Anmod, Dharwad, Hubballi, Davanagere, Chitradurga and Tumakuru to Bangalore. Group C returns via Belagavi and Kolhapur to Pune.
